About House of Wisdom

A place where education, faith, discipline and community come together.

Growing Through Faith & Education

House of Wisdom provides a supportive environment for Christian students studying in colleges and universities.

The hostel encourages students to develop their academic abilities while also growing in faith, responsibility, discipline and leadership.

Through prayer, community life, study and a structured daily routine, students are encouraged to prepare themselves for service and leadership.

"Forming Christian leaders who can serve God, the Church and society."
